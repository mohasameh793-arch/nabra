Questions

Is Nabra really free?
Yes. It’s open source under the MIT license — no trial, no subscription, no account.
Does it need the internet?
Only once, to download the speech and AI models during setup. After that everything runs offline on your PC.
What do I need to run it?
Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). It runs on any processor; for the fastest dictation and AI cleanup, an NVIDIA GPU with 10 GB+ memory is recommended. Setup picks the right models for your PC (about 1.6–10 GB).
Which Arabic dialects does it understand?
Egyptian, Gulf, Levantine and Modern Standard Arabic — plus English, French and many more. Choose your languages in Settings and switch between them freely, even mid-sentence.
Will it translate what I say?
No. Nabra writes exactly the language you spoke. If you want a translation, hold Right Alt and ask for one.
Which apps does it work in?
Any app where you can type — email, chat, browsers, Word, your code editor. The text appears where your cursor is.
How does the Notetaker work?
Press Ctrl+Alt+N (or the record button on the pill). Nabra transcribes your computer’s audio as “They” and your microphone as “You”, and can summarize the meeting when you’re done. No bot joins your call.
Is it on Mac or phone?
Not yet — Nabra is Windows-only for now. Contributions are welcome on GitHub.
