Speak naturally

Ramble, pause, switch between Arabic and English, change your mind mid-sentence. Nabra understands what you mean, not just what you said.

Edits as you speak

Fillers, repeats and false starts disappear. “Friday — no, Monday” becomes just “Monday”. Punctuation and spelling come out right.

Use it anywhere

Hold Right Ctrl in any app where you can type — email, chat, docs, your code editor. The text lands right at your cursor.

Arabic, English & more

Egyptian, Gulf, Levantine and MSA — plus English, French and more. Nabra follows you between languages, even mid-sentence, and never translates unless you ask.

Learns your vocabulary

Names, brands and the words of your trade. Add them once and Nabra spells them your way every time after.

Save your most used text

Snippets turn a short spoken cue into your email, address, booking link or a long sign-off.

Make Nabra sound like you

Pick a style per app — formal for email, casual for chat — and the same words come out in the right tone.

Your voice
stays yours.

Speech recognition and AI cleanup run on your own computer. There is no server to send your voice to, so nobody can read it, store it, or train on it.
